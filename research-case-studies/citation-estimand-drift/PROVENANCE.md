# Provenance of public reusable assets

Source: a private local exploratory workspace. Its name, manuscript metadata, private filenames and original
file hashes are intentionally withheld from this public repository.

The public scripts were reviewed and adapted to remove local absolute paths, credentials, restricted sources
and project-specific records. The public fixtures are synthetic and non-copyrighted; they are the only inputs
represented as runnable examples in this directory.

No unpublished manuscript or cover-letter file, restricted full-text PDF, publisher XML, private spreadsheet,
patient information, credential or private message is included. If private source assets are ever reused, their
identity, authorization, licence and hashes must be re-verified in the authorized private workspace rather than
reconstructed from this public repository.
