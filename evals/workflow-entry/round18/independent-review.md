## Agreed conclusion and boundary

- Both reviewers give candidate and baseline the same conservative system-level score, 9/10. No behavioral pass-rate improvement is claimed.
- Candidate is qualitatively more explicit about stable routing, source/contradiction ledgers, evidence status and commercial gates.
- The stable failure signal is commercial-role/economics completeness, especially payer separation in the baseline and unit economics under the stricter reading.
- Review covers written plans only. No live retrieval, parser run, full-text verification, customer interview, artifact generation, latency, token, or cost measurement occurred. It does not certify scientific correctness, root cause, privacy compliance, or commercial viability.
