# Synthetic Standards Council — Threshold Standard

Document: SSC-TS-3.0
Status: in force
Issued: 2026-04-01
Effective: 2026-05-01

## Section 2 — Scope

This synthetic standard applies only to assay Q measured with pipeline P3 in adults.

## Section 4 — Decision threshold

For the stated scope, classify a result as positive when Q is greater than or equal to 45 units.

## Section 8 — Supersession

SSC-TS-3.0 supersedes SSC-TS-2.1. It remains in force until a later final release is issued by the Council.
