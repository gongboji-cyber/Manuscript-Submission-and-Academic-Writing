# Synthetic Measurement Office — discrepancy note

Document: SMO-DN-1.1
Status: final methodological note
Issued: 2026-02-20

## Section 3 — Candidate explanations

A 100-fold difference between separately summarized batches can be produced by a unit conversion, by normalization to a different denominator, or by unrelated specimen composition. Aggregate means alone do not identify the cause.

## Section 4 — Minimum discriminating evidence

Compare paired values for the same specimens before and after processing, with recorded units, denominators, software version and processing log. A constant 100-fold ratio after matching may support a unit-conversion explanation; ratios that vary with the denominator may support normalization. These patterns remain hypotheses until the transformation record or equivalent provenance is verified.

## Section 6 — Reporting when provenance is absent

Report the observed discrepancy and state that its source cannot be determined from aggregate summaries. Do not assign a cause from magnitude alone.
