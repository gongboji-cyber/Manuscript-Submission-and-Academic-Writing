# Claim–source assessment

**Decision:** Not supported.

The supplied note reports one single-center randomized study of 120 adults, with a mortality risk ratio of 0.86 (95% CI, 0.64–1.17). Because the interval includes 1.00 and the note provides no subgroup, multicenter, or generalizability evidence, it does not support either a consistent mortality reduction or the claim’s extension to all adults.

**Evidence-aligned replacement sentence:** “In a single-center randomized study of 120 adults, the estimated mortality risk ratio for Treatment X was 0.86 (95% CI, 0.64–1.17).”
