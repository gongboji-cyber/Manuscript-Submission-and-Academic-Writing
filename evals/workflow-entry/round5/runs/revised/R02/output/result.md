# Claim–source assessment

**Verdict: Not supported.** The supplied note describes one single-center randomized study of 120 adults. Its mortality estimate, RR 0.86 (95% CI, 0.64–1.17), is compatible with both lower and higher mortality because the confidence interval includes 1. The note also supplies no subgroup, multicenter, or generalizability evidence. It therefore does not support a consistent mortality reduction or an effect in all adults. This judgment is limited to the supplied abstract-level note; no full text or supplement was available.

**Evidence-aligned replacement sentence:** “In a single-center randomized study of 120 adults, the estimated mortality risk ratio for Treatment X was 0.86 (95% CI, 0.64–1.17).”
