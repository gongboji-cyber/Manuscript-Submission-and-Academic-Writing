# Assessment

**Decision: Not supported.** The supplied note describes one single-center randomized study of 120 adults. Its mortality estimate (risk ratio 0.86; 95% CI, 0.64–1.17) is compatible with both a reduction and an increase in mortality, so it does not establish that Treatment X reduces mortality. The note also provides no subgroup, multicenter, or generalizability evidence to support “consistently” or “in all adults.”

**Evidence-aligned replacement:** “In this single-center randomized study of 120 adults, the estimated mortality risk ratio for Treatment X was 0.86 (95% CI, 0.64–1.17).”
